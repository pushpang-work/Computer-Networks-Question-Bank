## Question

Explain attenuation, distortion, and noise.

## Answer 

In data communication and signal processing, attenuation, distortion, and noise are the three main types of Transmission Impairment in Data Communication that cause a received signal to differ from the transmitted signal. 

### 1. Attenuation

* Definition: The gradual loss of signal strength (amplitude or energy) as it travels over a distance through a medium.
* Cause: Electrical resistance in the wire or medium, which converts some of the signal's energy into heat.
* Effect: The signal becomes weaker, making it harder for the receiver to detect or read.
* Unit of Measurement: Decibels (dB).
* Mitigation: Use amplifiers or repeaters to boost signal amplitude. 

### 2. Distortion

* Definition: An alteration in the original shape or waveform of the signal.
* Cause: In a composite signal (made of multiple frequencies), different frequency components travel at varying propagation speeds through the medium, causing them to arrive at the destination out of sync and altering phase relationships.
* Effect: The waveform deforms; frequency and phase relationships are changed, which can lead to data or bit errors.
* Mitigation: Use equalizers to adjust phase/frequency delays and select better transmission media. 

### 3. Noise

* Definition: Unwanted, random electrical or electromagnetic signals that mix with or corrupt the original signal. 
* Cause:
* Thermal noise: Random electron motion inside conductors.
   * Induced noise: External sources like motors or appliances.
   * Crosstalk: Signals bleeding from one wire to an adjacent wire.
   * Impulse noise: High-energy spikes from lightning or power lines. 
* Effect: Adds random corruption across the spectrum, reducing signal clarity. 
* Mitigation: Improve the Signal-to-Noise Ratio (SNR) using proper shielding, low-noise design, and filtering. 

### Quick Comparison

| Feature | Attenuation | Distortion | Noise |
|---|---|---|---|
| Core Nature | Loss of amplitude/energy | Change in signal shape/waveform | Addition of unwanted random signals |
| Shape Change? | No (waveform stays identical, just smaller) | Yes | Yes (random corruption) |
| Primary Fix | Amplifiers / Repeaters | Equalizers / Better media | Filters / Shielding / High SNR |
