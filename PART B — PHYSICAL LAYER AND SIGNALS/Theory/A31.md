## Question 

Calculate transmission time for a 12 MB file over a 24 Mbps link, ignoring overhead. State your unit assumptions.

## Answer 

The theoretical transmission time is 4 seconds.

### Formula
$\text{Transmission Time} = \frac{\text{File Size (in bits)}}{\text{Bandwidth (in bits per second)}}$

### Unit Assumptions

* Byte-to-Bit Conversion: 1 Megabyte (MB) = 8 Megabits (Mb). 
* Prefix System: Standard decimal metric prefixes are used, where "Mega" denotes $10^6$ ($1,000,000$).
    * File size: $12 \text{ MB} = 12 \times 10^6 \text{ Bytes}$
   * Link speed: $24 \text{ Mbps} = 24 \times 10^6 \text{ bits per second}$  

### Step-by-Step Calculation

1. Convert the file size to Megabits:

   $12 \text{ MB} \times 8 \text{ bits/Byte} = 96 \text{ Mb}$
   
2. Divide by the link speed:  
   
   $\text{Time} = \frac{96 \text{ Mb}}{24 \text{ Mbps}} = 4 \text{ seconds}$
