## Question 

Compare baseband and broadband transmission.

## Answer 

The main difference is that baseband transmission uses the entire bandwidth of a cable to send a single digital signal at a time, while broadband transmission uses modulation and frequency division to send multiple analog signals simultaneously over a single medium. 

### Key Differences

* Signal Type: Baseband uses digital signals (pulses or square waves), whereas broadband uses analog signals modulated onto higher carrier frequencies. 
* Number of Channels: Baseband carries one data stream (like a single-lane railway track). Broadband carries multiple channels at the same time (like a multi-lane highway). 
* Direction: Baseband is typically bidirectional (data flows both ways). Broadband is typically unidirectional per channel, meaning separate frequencies or cables are needed for sending and receiving.  
* Distance: Baseband signals degrade quickly and are limited to short distances (requiring repeaters). Broadband signals can travel long distances without significant attenuation (using amplifiers). 
* Multiplexing: Baseband uses Time Division Multiplexing (TDM), while broadband uses Frequency Division Multiplexing (FDM). 
* Common Uses: Baseband is used in Ethernet LANs and local data links. Broadband is used in cable TV, DSL, and long-haul telecommunications.
