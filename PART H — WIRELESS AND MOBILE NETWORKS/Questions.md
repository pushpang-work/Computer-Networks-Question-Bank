# PART H — WIRELESS AND MOBILE NETWORKS

### Theory questions
154. Explain Wi-Fi infrastructure mode, access points, stations, and SSIDs.
155. Compare 2.4 GHz, 5 GHz, and 6 GHz Wi-Fi bands in terms of range, capacity, and interference.
156. Explain channels, channel width, co-channel interference, and adjacent-channel interference.
157. Compare WPA2-Personal, WPA3-Personal, and enterprise Wi-Fi authentication.
158. Explain Bluetooth, BLE, Zigbee, and Thread at a high level.
159. Describe cellular generations from 2G through 5G and their broad design goals.
160. Explain roaming and handoff in wireless networks.
161. Compare infrastructure, ad hoc, and mesh wireless networks.
162. Explain the networking requirements of IoT devices.
163. Compare MQTT and CoAP and give a suitable use case for each.

### Practical tasks
164. Inspect Wi-Fi signal strength, link rate, channel, and security mode on your own device.
165. Create a Wi-Fi coverage map for a home or lab using measurements from several locations.
166. Design a secure guest Wi-Fi network separated from internal devices.
167. Build a small IoT messaging demonstration using an MQTT broker in an isolated lab.
168. Compare latency and packet loss over wired and wireless connections under similar conditions.
