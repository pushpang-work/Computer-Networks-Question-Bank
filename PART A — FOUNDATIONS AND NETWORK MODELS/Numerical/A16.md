## Question 

Draw a labelled diagram of a home network and identify the modem/ONT, router, switch, access point, clients, and Internet connection.

## Answer 

A standard home network flows from the outside internet through your service provider equipment to your local devices via wired and wireless paths.

### Component Breakdown

* Internet Connection: The external service provider (ISP) network delivering broadband via fiber, cable (coax), or DSL.
* Modem / ONT (Optical Network Terminal): Converts incoming ISP signals (light pulses from fiber or analog/digital signals from coax) into a standard Ethernet data stream. 
* Router: Connects to the modem/ONT, manages network traffic (routing and NAT), assigns local IP addresses via DHCP, and acts as your network's primary firewall. 
* Network Switch: Expands the number of physical Ethernet (wired) ports on your network to plug in stationary devices. 
* Wireless Access Point (AP): Broadcasts Wi-Fi signals to extend or enhance wireless coverage throughout the home (often built directly into the main router or deployed as standalone/mesh nodes).  
* Clients (End Devices): The personal and smart hardware using the network, split into wired clients (Desktop PCs, Smart TVs, NAS drives) and wireless clients (Laptops, Smartphones, Tablets).
